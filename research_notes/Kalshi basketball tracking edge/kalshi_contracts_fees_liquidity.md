# Kalshi sports event contracts (late 2026): basketball inventory, fees, liquidity, jurisdiction, API

Research date: 2026-11-01 (API snapshots pulled 2026-11-02 ~04:00 UTC, i.e. late evening ET on 2026-11-01, after the Nov 1 NBA slate and before the Nov 2 slate). Anything dated before mid-2026 is flagged "(pre-mid-2026)".

Method note: Kalshi's website and fee-schedule PDF returned HTTP 429 to the fetch tool, so the live inventory below comes from Kalshi's public, unauthenticated REST API (`https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/...`), which is the same data the website renders. Series tickers quoted verbatim from that API are primary evidence; counts are as of the snapshot and change daily.

## Key Question 1: Exact list of basketball competitions and contract types live on Kalshi now, and what was added in 2025-2026

### Takeaway
As of 2026-11-02 Kalshi lists 4,076 sports "series" (market families), 721 of them tagged Basketball, spanning NBA, WNBA, NCAA men's and women's, ~40 international leagues (EuroLeague, EuroCup, FIBA competitions, ACB, BBL, Serie A, BSL, LKL, CBA, KBL, B.League, etc.), Olympics, Summer League, Unrivaled, BIG3 and streamer events; there is no G League series. For the NBA the menu covers game winner, spread, total, team total, every quarter/half (winner/spread/total/team total), 15+ player-prop stat families, combos/parlays, futures, awards and "specials." The build-out happened in stages: single-game moneylines (Jan 2025), spreads/totals/NFL props (Aug 2025), Combos beta (Sept 2025) and full launch (Dec 2025), NBA player props (Nov 2025).

### Inferences
- The series list is a superset of what is open on any given night: e.g. WNBA, Unrivaled, Summer League and pre-packaged NBA combos had 0 open markets at snapshot because they are out of season or seasonal promos. Treat series existence as "Kalshi has listed this at some point and can relist it."
- Duplicate tickers (`KXNBA1H` vs `KXNBA1HWINNER`, `KXNBAOT` vs `KXNBAOVERTIME`, `KXNBAFINMVP` vs `KXNBAFINALSMVP`) indicate ticker migrations; a tracker should resolve by title, not ticker, and should prefer the shorter/newer ticker that currently has open markets.
- Market-type coverage for NBA is now at sportsbook parity on pre-game menus (ML/spread/total/team total/periods/props/SGP-like combos). Depth, not breadth, is the gap (see Q4).

### Gaps
- Could not fetch kalshi.com's category pages directly (HTTP 429), so I cannot confirm the exact on-site section names/counts beyond the search-index snippet.
- No source states the exact date NBA spreads/totals (as opposed to NFL) went live; they were present by the Nov 2025 prop launch, likely rolled out in fall 2025, but I found no dated announcement.
- No evidence either way on Australian NBL or Philippine PBA series; not seen in the filtered list, but the filter was keyword-based.

## Key Question 2: Does Kalshi offer player props and in-game (live, mid-game) markets for basketball? For which leagues?

### Takeaway
Yes on both. Player props exist for NBA (15+ stat families) and WNBA (points, rebounds, assists, threes, H2H); none were found for NCAA men's or women's or international leagues (only team-level "race to points"/"first three" markets). In-game trading is structural: every market keeps trading until close, and Kalshi additionally lists quarter/half winner-spread-total markets for NBA, WNBA, NCAA men's (halves) and NCAA women's (quarters and halves). The pending CFTC rule (June 2026 proposal) would likely bar "discrete-action contracts involving specific participants" (in-game props), so prop structure is at regulatory risk.

### Cited Findings
- NBA player prop series present at snapshot: PTS, REB, AST, 3PT, BLK, STL, STOCKS, FTM, PRA, PR, PA, RA, double-double, triple-double, ladders/escalators, protected variants, H2H points/PRA/threes, first basket, game points leader. — [Kalshi public API](https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/series?category=Sports)
- WNBA player prop series: `KXWNBAPTS`, `KXWNBAREB`, `KXWNBAAST`, `KXWNBA3PT`, `KXWNBAH2HPTS`, `KXWNBAH2HPRA`, `KXWNBA40PTS`, `KXWNBADUNK`. — same source
- NCAA men's/women's: no per-player stat series found; only team/game-level in-game style markets (`KXNCAAMBFIRST10/25/50`, `KXNCAAMBFIRST3PT`, `KXNCAAMBRACE`, `KXNCAAWBRACE`, `KXNCAAWBFIRST3PT`) plus season-long `KXNCAAMBSTATLEADER`/`KXNCAAWBSTATLEADER` and tournament `KXMARMADPTS`/`KXMARMAD3D`. — same source

### Inferences
- Kalshi's props are "N+" threshold ladders (e.g. 20+/25+/30+ points), not a single over/under line, so a prop "market" maps to several binary contracts per player per stat. Tracking tools should key on (event, player, stat, threshold).
- "Protected" prop variants (`…PROT`) most likely refund/void if the player does not play (a DNP-protection feature); I could not find the rule text and flag this as an inference.
- Whether the NBA prop per-trade cap of $10,000 still applies is unknown; no 2026 source restates it.

### Gaps
- No primary source describes how Kalshi handles props during the game (whether new thresholds are added mid-game, or whether quotes are pulled); only the Blockworks characterisation of makers thinning size.
- No source confirms NCAA player props are deliberately excluded (plausibly for NCAA-integrity reasons, cf. FOS reporting on transfer-portal markets), only their absence in the API.

## Key Question 3: Fee schedule with the formula and worked examples at 50-cent and 90-cent prices; maker rebates if any

### Takeaway
Taker fee = round-up-to-cent( M × 0.07 × C × P × (1−P) ); maker fee = round-up( M × 0.0175 × C × P × (1−P) ), but the maker multiplier is zero on most series and non-zero only on series flagged "with maker fees" — which, per the API, includes the main basketball game/spread/total series (NBA, NCAA men's, WNBA) and NBA/NCAA futures and awards, while props, period markets, NCAA women's and international leagues carry no maker fee. There are no maker rebates and no volume-based fee tiers; MLB series have a 0.5 multiplier.

### Cited Findings
- Taker formula: fees = round up(0.07 × C × P × (1−P)), C = contracts, P = price in dollars, rounded up to the next cent; maker formula uses 0.0175, applied only on series listed with maker fees; cancelling a resting order costs nothing. — [BettingUSA Kalshi fees (verified Aug 31 2026, updated Oct 28 2026)](https://www.bettingusa.com/prediction-markets/reviews/kalshi/fees/); [dev.to fee explainer](https://dev.to/polytrage/kalshis-fee-structure-explained-55lg); [Allium](https://allium.so/blog/kalshi-fees-why-the-cost-peaks-near-50-cents/)
- Worked examples (BettingUSA table, standard multiplier 1): 100 contracts at $0.50 → 0.07 × 100 × 0.5 × 0.5 = $1.75 taker; 1 contract at $0.50 → $0.0175 rounds up to $0.02. At $0.90: 100 contracts → 0.07 × 100 × 0.9 × 0.1 = $0.63; 1 contract → $0.0063 rounds up to $0.01. — [BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/prediction-markets/reviews/kalshi/fees/)
- Maker worked examples (same formula with 0.0175, where applicable): 100 at $0.50 → $0.4375 → displayed/charged $0.44; 100 at $0.90 → $0.1575 → $0.16. — [dev.to (notes schedule shows whole cents, $0.4375 appears as $0.44)](https://dev.to/polytrage/kalshis-fee-structure-explained-55lg)
- Fee curve peaks at 50c (1.75c/contract taker) and falls toward the extremes (0.63c at 10c or 90c). — [Whirligigbear, "Maker/Taker Math on Kalshi", Sept 17 2025 (pre-mid-2026)](https://whirligigbear.substack.com/p/makertaker-math-on-kalshi); [Allium](https://allium.so/blog/kalshi-fees-why-the-cost-peaks-near-50-cents/)
- Which series charge maker fees (API `fee_type` field, snapshot 2026-11-02): 107 of 4,076 sports series are `quadratic_with_maker_fees`; the other 3,969 are `quadratic` (maker multiplier 0). Basketball series with maker fees: `KXNBAGAME`, `KXNBASPREAD`, `KXNBATOTAL`, `KXNBASERIES`, `KXNBA` (champion), `KXNBAEAST`, `KXNBAWEST`, `KXNBAMVP`, `KXNBAROY`, `KXNBACOY`, `KXNBAFINALSMVP`, `KXNCAAMBGAME`, `KXNCAAMBSPREAD`, `KXNCAAMBTOTAL`, `KXMARMAD`, `KXWNBAGAME`, `KXWNBA`. Other sports with maker fees include `KXNFLGAME`, `KXNCAAFGAME/SPREAD/TOTAL`, `KXMLBGAME`, `KXNHLGAME`, `KXEPLGAME`, `KXLALIGAGAME`, `KXUCLGAME`, `KXBUNDESLIGAGAME`, `KXWTAMATCH`, Grand Slam singles, `KXSB`, `KXNFLMVP`. NBA props, quarter/half markets, team totals, NCAA women's and EuroLeague series are plain `quadratic`. — [Kalshi public API, series endpoint](https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/series?category=Sports)
- Fee multiplier field: all basketball series show `fee_multiplier` 1; 19 MLB series (`KXMLBGAME`, `KXMLBSPREAD`, `KXMLBTOTAL`, `KXMLBF5`, `KXMLBKS`, `KXMLBHR`, etc.) show 0.5, i.e. half the standard fee. — same source
- Combos: one third-party source says combo markets (excluding uncorrelated NFL combos) charge maker fees at 50% of the taker fee. — [DeFi Rate fee guide (secondary, unverified against Kalshi PDF)](https://defirate.com/prediction-markets/fees/)
- Deposit/withdrawal: withdrawals 0% on all methods; ACH deposits 0%; PayPal/Venmo first deposit free then up to 2%; debit card/Apple Pay/Google Pay/Cash App/wire up to 2%. — [BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/prediction-markets/reviews/kalshi/fees/)
- Interest: Kalshi pays ~4% on account balances/positions (per Sept 2025 article; pre-mid-2026). — [Whirligigbear](https://whirligigbear.substack.com/p/makertaker-math-on-kalshi)
- No source — official or third-party — mentions a maker rebate (negative fee) or volume-tiered trading fees. Volume tiers exist only for API rate limits (see Q6). — [docs.kalshi.com rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits); [BettingUSA](https://www.bettingusa.com/prediction-markets/reviews/kalshi/fees/)
- InGame estimated Kalshi's fee take on the 2026 NCAA tournaments at $28.5M on $2.18B non-parlay college basketball volume (~1.3% of volume), using the published fee schedule. — [InGame, Apr 9 2026](https://www.ingame.com/2026-ncaa-tournament-volume-fees-kalshi/)

### Inferences
- Effective round-trip cost for a taker on an NBA game winner at ~50c: ~1.75c in plus ~1.75c out = ~3.5c on a $1 contract, before the 1–2c bid-ask spread; at 90c the round trip is ~1.3c. For high-frequency strategies, posting (maker) on `quadratic` series (props, quarters) is fee-free, whereas on `KXNBAGAME/SPREAD/TOTAL` a maker still pays ~0.44c per contract at 50c.
- Because fees round up per order (not per contract), small retail orders pay a much higher effective rate: 1 contract at 50c pays 2c (4% of notional) versus 1.75% for 100 contracts.
- The MLB 0.5 multiplier shows Kalshi uses `fee_multiplier` as a promotional lever by sport; the field should be read live from the API rather than hard-coded.

## Key Question 6: API — endpoints for sports markets, websocket feeds, order rate limits, data latency

### Takeaway
Kalshi has a public, documented REST + WebSocket + FIX trading API (`docs.kalshi.com`, base `https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2`); market data is unauthenticated, orders need RSA-signed requests. Rate limits are token-bucket per tier (Basic: 200 read / 100 write tokens per second, default 10 tokens per request, i.e. ~20 reads/s and ~10 writes/s; up to 12,000/9,600 at the top tier), with tiers above Advanced earned by trailing-30-day volume share. WebSocket channels cover order-book deltas, ticker, public trades, fills, market lifecycle and multivariate lifecycle; Basic accounts may hold 200 concurrent WS connections. 429s carry no Retry-After header. No official latency SLA is published.

### Cited Findings
- Rate-limit model: token buckets; each request costs 10 tokens by default (`GET /account/endpoint_costs` lists exceptions); separate Read and Write buckets; `GET /portfolio/balance` has its own bucket (1/s Basic and Advanced, 5/s higher tiers); REST and FIX share buckets. Predictions budgets (tokens/s, read/write): Basic 200/100; Advanced 300/300; Expert 600/600; Premier 1,200/1,200; Paragon 2,400/2,400; Prime 4,800/4,800; Prestige 12,000/9,600. Basic/Advanced read buckets and write buckets above Basic hold up to 3 s of budget (burst); others 1 s. Write budgets are shard-scoped (orders routed to shard ≥1 draw from that shard's bucket); batch create/cancel always bill the unscoped write bucket and each item is billed separately. 429 returns `{"error":"too many requests"}` with no Retry-After or X-RateLimit headers; no cooldown penalty; exponential backoff recommended. — [docs.kalshi.com, Rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits)
- Tier progression: Basic on signup; Advanced via the "Upgrade Account API Usage Level" endpoint; Expert–Prestige earned by volume share = trailing-30-day volume ÷ (2 × previous month's exchange volume): Expert earn 0.075% / keep 0.05%; Premier 0.125/0.10; Paragon 0.25/0.20; Prime 0.50/0.40; Prestige 1.00/0.80; grants last 30 days and renew. — [docs.kalshi.com, Rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits)
- WebSocket connection limits (concurrent, shared Predictions + Perps): Basic/Advanced/Expert 200; Premier 500; Paragon 750; Prime/Prestige 1,000; excess handshakes get 429. — [docs.kalshi.com, Rate limits](https://docs.kalshi.com/getting_started/rate_limits)

- Third-party WS details (unverified against the 404'd intro page): production endpoint `wss://external-api-ws.kalshi.com/trade-api/ws/v2` (demo `wss://external-api-ws.demo.kalshi.co/trade-api/ws/v2`); subscribe message `{"id":1,"cmd":"subscribe","params":{"channels":["fill","order_book_delta"],"market_tickers":["..."]}}`; auth headers on the HTTP upgrade; silent disconnect after ~60 s idle (recommend ping 20 s / timeout 10 s); no replay of missed frames — resubscribe and re-request a snapshot after reconnect. — [DEV Community bot-builder guide (self-reported check against docs Oct 23, 2026)](https://dev.to/deskbot_store/kalshi-api-rate-limits-and-websockets-a-bot-builders-guide-4kj5)
- Sports market data used in this research was retrieved without authentication from `GET /series?category=Sports` and `GET /markets?series_ticker=…&status=open&limit=200` (max page size 200, cursor pagination). Market objects expose `yes_bid_dollars`, `yes_ask_dollars`, `yes_bid_size_fp`, `yes_ask_size_fp`, `last_price_dollars`, `volume_fp`, `volume_24h_fp`, `open_interest_fp`, `price_level_structure` (`linear_cent`), `price_ranges` (step 0.01, sub-penny on some futures), `rules_primary/secondary`, `expected_expiration_time`, `settlement_timer_seconds`, and sports-specific `custom_strike` (e.g. `basketball_team` UUID). Series objects expose `fee_type`, `fee_multiplier`, `tags`, `frequency`. Legacy integer-cent fields (`yes_bid`, `volume`, `open_interest`) are no longer populated on these endpoints. — [Kalshi public API](https://api.elections.kalshi.com/trade-api/v2/markets?series_ticker=KXNBAGAME&status=open) (observed 2026-11-02)

### Inferences
- For a basketball tracking bot on a Basic tier: ~20 REST reads/s and ~10 order actions/s sustained (with 3-s burst on reads), which is ample for polling a few hundred markets if you use the multi-orderbook and batch candlestick endpoints; use WebSocket `orderbook_delta`/`ticker` for in-game pricing rather than polling.
- Advanced tier (300/300) is a free one-call upgrade; higher tiers require measurable volume share (Expert ≈ 0.075% of 2× monthly exchange volume, i.e. on the order of tens of millions of dollars/month at current exchange volumes).

### Gaps
- `docs.kalshi.com/websockets/introduction` returned 404 and the `.md` page variants were not fetched, so channel names and message schemas are from the docs index titles and a third-party guide, not the official page text.
- Kalshi publishes no latency figures, no market-data SLA, and no documented per-order-size limits in the pages retrieved.
